Gym: pick a direction (all dark)

The new look for Gym, built from 7 Nov together with Part B. Each phone shows the new Today home from the Part B plan: calories and macros left, the next planned meal with one-tap Eaten, today's workout and the weigh-in. Sample numbers: your 2100 kcal target (193 g protein, 56 g fat, 207 g carbs), part of the day eaten. Every direction keeps a dark look, as you asked, and works the same on the Mac.

DIRECTION 1 · MY RECOMMENDATION

Discos

Competition bumper plates on a rubber gym floor. The calibrated plate colours (red 25, blue 20, yellow 15, green 10) become the app's palette: protein is red, carbs blue, fat yellow, training green. Today's macros stand as three plates seen edge on, filling as you eat. Numbers are set in a heavy condensed type, like the numbers moulded into a plate.

Feels
Physical, strong, a real gym. Black rubber, saturated plate colour, chalk-white numbers.
Signature
The three macro plates: one look tells you what's left of each.
Training
Sets and PRs use the plate colours, and the plate calculator becomes part of the look.
Risk
Four saturated colours need discipline: they carry data, never decoration.

DIRECTION 2

Cronos

A Formula 1 timing tower: black, tight condensed numbers in one tabular column, and the timing colours that every racing fan reads instantly. Purple is a personal best, green is better than last time, yellow is a warning. Today reads like a lap: the day's numbers stacked with their deltas, and each lift's set marked purple when it's a PR.

Feels
Fast, precise, competitive. The densest of the three.
Signature
Purple, green and yellow deltas on everything that can improve (weight trend, lifts, protein streak).
Training
The live workout becomes a timing tower of sets, with a purple flash on a PR.
Risk
Dense; must stay calm on food days with nothing to "race".

DIRECTION 3

Jueces

The three referee lights at a lifting meet: white means good lift. Each day gets three lights: Eat (calories in your band), Protein (target hit) and Train or rest (the plan for today done). They light up as the day goes, filling as each gets closer. Black ground, white light, almost no colour: the calmest and most minimal.

Feels
Serious, quiet, a clear verdict. "Three white lights" becomes the daily goal.
Signature
Today's three lights, and a strip of past days' lights in Progress.
Training
Each set gets its own light when logged; a PR adds a gold edge.
Risk
Very little colour; macros need their small colour dots to stay apart.
