# Gym

A private nutrition and training app for iPhone and Mac, built for lifters. It's modelled on
MacroFactor's coaching, with its own take on logging, training and design.

- **Nutrition:** fast logging (search, barcode, nutrition-label scan, AI photo with dishes broken into ingredients, quick add), a timeline, copy/move between days.
- **Coaching:** one weight trend and expenditure from the coaching engine (the same numbers on screen and behind your targets), weekly check-ins with guardrails, programs (coached, collaborative, manual), phases, refeeds, diet breaks, and a daily AI coaching note.
- **Training:** routines, live workouts with a rest timer on the lock screen, progression, PRs, volume per muscle.
- **Body:** weigh-ins with scale tags, measurements, progress photos.
- **Yours:** data stays on your devices; the Mac and iPhone sync directly over Tailscale; daily backups; optional Face ID lock.
- **Meal planner:** plans each day's meals to your targets from foods you eat; swap, keep, servings in units.
- **Look:** Appearance (Automatic, Light, Dark, Black) × Finish (Titanium, Silver, Graphite, Gold, Rose Gold).
